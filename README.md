# SecureHealth Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) Strategy

## 🛡️ Project Overview
As a Junior Cybersecurity Analyst at **SecureHealth**—a mid-sized healthcare organization specializing in electronic medical record (EMR) software and telehealth services—this project establishes a proactive Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) strategy to combat rising ransomware threats, protect patient privacy, and ensure regulatory compliance (HIPAA).

---

## 🎯 Threat Intelligence Objectives
* **Enhance Ransomware Detection & Mitigation:** Detect and contain ransomware attacks prior to network-wide propagation using advanced threat feeds and endpoint detection.
* **Protect Patient Data & Ensure Compliance:** Implement strict security measures around EMR systems to maintain full alignment with HIPAA and healthcare regulations.
* **Improve Incident Response Capabilities:** Strengthen operational continuity protocols during security incidents using integrated recovery tools.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Environment & Stack
* **Threat Intelligence Platform (TIP):** Recorded Future (for threat feed integration and proactive analysis)
* **Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR):** CrowdStrike (for endpoint security and real-time incident response)
* **Data Backup & Recovery:** Veeam (for frequent backups to minimize downtime and ensure business continuity)

---

## 📋 Report Structure & Key Areas Covered
The accompanying report covers the following core components of the CTI framework:
1. **Define Objectives and Scope:** Outlining organizational goals, technological boundaries, and policy alignment.
2. **Threat Intelligence Needs Assessment:** Analyzing stakeholder requirements across IT Security, Compliance, and Incident Response teams.
3. **Gather and Integrate Intelligence Sources:** Utilizing Recorded Future, open-source intelligence (OSINT), and industry-specific feeds.
4. **Develop Threat Intelligence Processes:** Establishing structured workflows for data collection, analytical processing, and tactical/strategic dissemination.
5. **Build CTI Capabilities and Infrastructure:** Integrating tooling (CrowdStrike, Veeam) with intelligence feeds.
6. **Establish Communication and Collaboration Channels:** Defining reporting frameworks and cross-departmental communication protocols.
7. **Measure CTI Effectiveness & Optimize Strategy:** Key performance indicators (KPIs) to evaluate intelligence ROI and defense posture improvements.
